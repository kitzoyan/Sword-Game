Here's a huge new feature I'd like added. It's called Arts.
Characters can use special moves called Arts to change the combat pattern and deal more damage.
Arts start off costing 50 stamina (half of the stamina bar), and recovers by cool down.
For every 15 seconds that passes during actual combat (developer mode not included), the stamina and cool down
time of arts decrease by some amount, up until a minimum limit. This rewards combatants for surviving longer battles, and makes the game more heated.
For now, let's start off with 4 individual arts, whose cool downs are counted seperately from other arts.
When an art is parried, the user of the art will NOT be staggered. Additionally, characters who parry arts are "knocked back" by a certain distance.
This mechanism encourages combatants to dodge arts in order to close the gap and punish opponents.
Each art will follow 6 animation states/frames. As such, for implementation, the animation logic/ladder for arts should be a separate "if" block from light, heavy, and charge attack animations.
The animation states are as follows: A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A6. NOTE THAT NONE OF THE STATES HAVE AN ACTIVE HITBOX. The combatant cannot be HIT during an art, and the combatant's character model also does not have attack-hitboxes.
Only sprites produced from the arts have hitboxes.
The GUI for arts will be diamond shaped (square but 45 degrees) and will sit beside the health bar of the player.
The visual indication that a combatant is about to use an art will be the shining-flash sprite used for heavy and charged attacks, plus additional sparks effect, as follows:
- The shining-flash will appear at the sword tip on state A1
- White sparks (the same used for parrying) will surge out of the combatant on state A1.
- Enable a mode called DYNAMIC CAMERA which when toggled, changes the camera position and angle only for states A1, A2, and A3. These should be tunable values.

Now, for the actual art abilities themselves:
ART 1 - CENTIPEDE: A massive horizontal 360 degrees singular strike, producing a circular air slash (a white ring sprite) that starts around the user and expands incredibly fast outwards. Range should cover a radius of roughly half the stage. There is only one active hitbox that travles with the ring.
ART 2 - KAGURA: An singular aerial attack that produces multiple slashes SPHERE shaped around the user in a single instance. Using multiple of the same white ring sprite, the radius of the attack is much more local to the user. There is only one hit box that travels with the sphere surface.
ART 3 - HARMONIC: An aerieal attack that has two seperate air slashes (a white crescent shape) that travel diagonally down towards the opponents last-known position. The slashes are not homing, and they are fired one after the other. Each slash has their own hit box that travels with them.
ART 4 - OVERCLOCK: A rotating horizontal flip that deals two instances of air slashes (white crescent). The slashes are not homing, and they do not travel much. They are fired in order, and each have their own hit box. This attack is considered a melee attack.
